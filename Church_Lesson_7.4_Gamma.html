"CHURCH"
The Imperfect
& Chosen

"The church was never advertised as a museum for the perfect. It was always a family of sinners saved by grace."

Big Idea: The Church is a family of sinners saved by grace, called to represent God's kingdom on earth.

"But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people belonging to God, that you may declare the praises of Him who called you out of darkness into His wonderful light."

— 1 Peter 2:9

Hook

"I Love God, Just Not the Church"

Take a moment to reflect:

  • Have you ever thought the church is full of hypocrites — people who look one way on Sunday and live differently the rest of the week?
  • Have you ever been hurt by someone in a church, or watched someone you love walk away because of it?
  • Have you ever judged an entire church — or the whole Church — by one bad experience?

Today we're looking at what the Church actually is — and it's not what a lot of us picture, and honestly, it's not what some of us have experienced either.

The Misconception

"The Church Is Full of Hypocrites"

The Lie

All Christians are fake so the whole church must be fake too.

Matthew 15:7-9

"You hypocrites!... These people honor Me with their lips, but their hearts are far from Me. They worship Me in vain; their teachings are but rules taught by men."

Matthew 23:27-28

"Woe to you... you hypocrites! You are like whitewashed tombs, which look beautiful on the outside but... are full of hypocrisy and wickedness."

Here's the catch: the Bible doesn't deny this. Jesus said it first, and was harder than any critic of the church ever has (1 John 3:10 This is how we know who the children of God are and who the children of the devil are: Anyone who does not do what is right is not God’s child, nor is anyone who does not love their brother and sister).


Which means the real question isn't whether hypocrisy is real — it's whether hypocrisy is the whole story.

The church was never a museum for saints. It's a family for people who know they need grace.

Ephesians 2:4-5

"But because of His great love for us, God, who is rich in mercy, made us alive with Christ even when we were dead in transgressions — it is by grace you have been saved."

Colossians 1:13-14

"He has rescued us from the dominion of darkness and brought us into the kingdom of the Son He loves, in whom we have redemption, the forgiveness of sins."

Ephesians 4:32

"Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as in Christ God forgave you."

Biblical Example

The Day a Family Was Born

The church is also a place to find your home and family.

The Spark

The Holy Spirit arrived loudly, drawing a large crowd. (Acts 2:1-6)

The Sermon

Peter, filled with the Spirit, preached that Jesus really was the Messiah. (2:14-36)

The Response

About 3,000 people believed and were baptized that day. The Church was born. (vv.38-41)

The Family

They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching, ate together, prayed together, and shared everything they had. (vv.42-47)

Peter told everyone the why of their belief and why they do what they do. To help those in need and be a light in the darkness. To go and "build a church" everywhere they go.

What It Means

What "Church" Actually Means

Ecclesia

A congregation or government structure.

The Church serves as the representative of God's kingdom on earth, his structure and values.

Every person in it, from day one, was guilty of sin deserving punishment (Romans 3:23). Each one was forgiven the moment they trusted Christ, and received the Holy Spirit the moment they believed (Acts 2:38).

On this rock build my Church (Matthew 16:18)

The Church isn't the four walls. We are commanded to build disciples to show the world the Love of Jesus to share his light back to the Earth so that we can all follow the values that Christ Jesus had. 

That's still who the Church is today: a huge family of sinful people saved by grace, given power to live a new life led by God's Spirit (Romans 8:1-11).

Modern Examples

What Made Them Magnetic

The First Church

Devoted to the apostles' teaching, ate together in homes, shared with anyone in need, praised God with glad hearts — and people joined them daily. (vv.42-47)

Our Reputation Today

Cliques, politics, and a gap between what we say and how we live — the very things that make people say "I love God, just not the church."

Not every church functions like Acts 2 — and honestly, life got harder for that first church too. But we still can, and should, be God's representatives for others to see how His kingdom brings joy, generosity, and purpose into the lives of those who trust Him.

Broken and Chosen

Broken and Chosen

The World Says

"The church is just a bunch of fake, judgmental hypocrites."

God Says

"You are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people belonging to God." (1 Peter 2:9)

The balance: Hypocrisy in the church is real and worth naming — Jesus named it first. But it was never the last word. God's verdict on this same imperfect group is "chosen." Both things are true at once.

Identity Shift

From Critic to Family Member

Stop judging the family from the doorway. Step in and help carry it.

You're not a customer grading the church's performance from a distance. You're part of what makes it what it is — imperfections included.

Action Steps

How to Actually Be the Church

1

Examine Your Own Heart First

Before pointing at hypocrisy out there, ask where you're inconsistent with what you claim to believe (1 John 1:9).

2

Forgive the Person Who Hurt You

Hold believers responsible for real harm, but forgive as you've been forgiven (Ephesians 4:32).

3

Get Involved, Not Just Critical

Don't just critique the church from the outside — join the family and help carry it.

4

Share What Church Has Done For You

Someone needs to hear that church can help, not just hurt.

5

Represent the Kingdom On Purpose

Let your life be evidence, not just your words (1 Peter 2:9).

Church · Imperfect and Chosen10
Reflection

Are You Holding a Grudge?

1

Are You Holding a Grudge?

Are you holding a grudge against the church because of the actions of fellow believers? Do you need to forgive them?

2

Where Are You Inconsistent?

The world often sees the Church as a bunch of hypocrites. They may be wrong about a lot, but they're not making it up out of nowhere. How are we acting in ways inconsistent with the teachings of Jesus? Do we have sin we need to confess (1 John 1:9)?

Worth knowing: Researchers who study forgiveness have found that holding onto a grudge keeps your body in a stress response — elevated cortisol, rumination, worse sleep — long after the original offense is over. Unforgiveness doesn't punish the person who hurt you. It keeps punishing you.

This Week's Challenge: Either tell someone your own story of how the church helped you — or if you're holding a grudge, take one concrete step toward forgiving it.

Guilty and chosen. Broken and beloved. That's not a contradiction — that's the whole Church.
